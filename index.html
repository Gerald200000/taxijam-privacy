Taxi Rank Jam – Privacy Policy

Pureform Solutions · Effective 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Taxi Rank Jam (the "app"), a puzzle game for Android made by Pureform Solutions ("we", "us"). In short: we don't ask you for any personal information and we don't run our own servers. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to deliver them, as described below.

1. Information the app stores on your device

To remember your game, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your current level and coin balance
  • whether background music is switched on or off

This information is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no contact forms. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear as a banner during play and as optional video ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward (continuing a level, doubling coins, or a free hint).

To show and measure these ads, Google's software in the app may collect and process information such as:

InformationUsed for
Device advertising ID (Android advertising ID)Showing, limiting and measuring ads; personalised ads where you allow them

The app may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features (Topics and Attribution) where your device supports them, which Google uses for ad selection and measurement.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and personal data

4. Your choices and consent

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it, so you can choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads on some devices). You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch a video ad to play the game.
  • Your device data: uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

5. Children

Taxi Rank Jam is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help you have it removed.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled as described in section 3.

7. Security and retention

Game progress is kept on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data transmitted by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit and retained by Google according to its policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA, the EU and UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google using the links in section 3. You are welcome to contact us with any question about your privacy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app gains new features. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
